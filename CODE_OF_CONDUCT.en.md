# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

> [简体中文 (默认)](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Our Standards

Positive behaviors include constructive feedback, focusing on reproducible evidence, test determinism, and user safety. Unacceptable behaviors include harassment, publishing private credentials/internal system details without authorization, or intentionally submitting `test.skip`/`test.fixme` workarounds to bypass quality gates.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to repository maintainers via GitHub Security Advisories and will be reviewed promptly and fairly.
